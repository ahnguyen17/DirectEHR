# Product Context

**Purpose:**
This EHR application aims to provide healthcare professionals with a streamlined system for managing patient data and clinical workflows.

**Problems Solved:**
- Centralized patient information access.
- Efficient recording of patient interactions and data.
- Improved organization of patient records.

**How it Should Work:**
- Intuitive user interface for navigation and data entry.
- Secure storage and retrieval of sensitive patient information.
- Clear presentation of patient history, vitals, notes, and orders.

**User Experience Goals:**
- Easy and fast data entry.
- Quick access to critical patient information.
- Minimal training required for healthcare professionals.
